## 📖 Course Overview

**ABE 721: Agricultural Systems Modelling and Simulation** (Advanced Computer Aided Design and Analysis) is a rigorous postgraduate course designed for master's students at Sokoine University of Agriculture. It synthesizes mathematical modeling, numerical discretization, finite element analysis (FEA), and dynamic biological simulation to analyze complex agricultural and environmental systems.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience & Degree Programs

This course provides advanced analytical modeling frameworks for students in:

1. **MSc in Irrigation Engineering and Management (MSc IEM)**:
   * **Plant-Water Relations & Dynamic Soil Moisture**: Discretizing continuous unsaturated water flow in soils using Richards' equation and Forward Euler/Runge-Kutta numerical integration.
   * **Soft Computing in Water Resources**: Applying Artificial Neural Networks (ANN), Fuzzy Logic, and Genetic Algorithms to predict streamflow, evapotranspiration, and canal conveyance losses.
   * **Hydraulic System Dynamics**: Causal Loop Diagrams (CLD) and stock-flow architectures for reservoir storage, irrigation demand cycles, and aquifer depletion.

2. **MSc in Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering (MSc ABE)**:
   * **Dynamic Crop & Biological Systems**: Multi-compartment dynamic models for crop growth, biomass accumulation, and nutrient uptake.
   * **Finite Element Analysis (FEA)**: Structural stress analysis, matrix displacement methods, and thermal distribution in agricultural machinery and storage structures using MATLAB and CAD/CAM tools.
   * **System Dynamics**: Analyzing feedback loops in agro-ecosystems, energy balances in post-harvest drying, and greenhouse microclimates.

## 📋 Course Policies & Submission Guidelines

* **LaTeX Requirement**: All assignments and term papers must be typeset in $\LaTeX$ (preferably using Overleaf) and submitted as PDF. Handwritten work is not accepted.
* **Code Submission**: Students must provide both properly documented $\LaTeX$ explanations (with embedded output plots) and executable source code (`.py`, `.m`, or `.R`).
