## 📖 Course Overview

**AEC 813: Artificial Intelligence, IoT, and GIS Application in Agriculture** is a specialized doctoral-level course designed for PhD candidates in Agroecology and advanced engineering researchers. The course synthesizes emerging frontier technologies—Artificial Intelligence, Internet of Things (IoT) wireless sensor networks, and Geographic Information Systems (GIS)—into cohesive data-driven frameworks for sustainable agriculture, climate adaptation, and biosystems resource optimization.

---

## 🎯 Research Applications by Domain

* **Agroecology & Biodiversity**: Geospatial tracking of ecological corridors, landscape connectivity, species habitat suitability, and pest-predator population dynamics using spatial GIS layers.
* **Precision Irrigation & Hydrology**: Large-scale catchment telemetry, remote sensing evapotranspiration mapping, and AI-driven predictive allocation of surface and groundwater resources.
* **Smart Farming & Edge AI**: Embedded deep learning models running on low-power microcontrollers (Edge Impulse, TinyML) for in-field pest identification, weed classification, and crop stress diagnostics.
* **Digital Twins**: Multi-source data assimilation combining physical soil-water models, real-time IoT sensor streams, and satellite imagery into real-time agricultural digital twins.

| **Lecture 4** | **AI & Machine Learning**: CNNs for vision, RNN/LSTMs for weather forecasting, and unsupervised clustering. |
