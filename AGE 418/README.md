## 📖 Course Overview

**AGE 418: Sensors and Controls in Precision Agriculture** is the premier senior capstone course offered by the Department of Agricultural Engineering at Sokoine University of Agriculture. Delivered by **Dr. Kadeghe Goodluck Fue** through the **Electronics and Precision Agriculture Lab (EPAL)**, the course prepares graduating engineers to implement **Agriculture 4.0** technologies that address spatial and temporal variability across agricultural landscapes.

The curriculum integrates satellite positioning (GNSS/RTK), Geographic Information Systems (GIS), satellite and drone remote sensing, proximal soil and crop sensing, yield monitoring, Variable Rate Technology (VRT), automated control systems, and agricultural robotics.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience & Degree Program Competencies

1. **BSc in Agricultural Engineering (BSc AE — Year 4)**:
   * **Machine Auto-Guidance**: Working with Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) GNSS for sub-centimeter automated steering and implement hitch control.
   * **Variable Rate Application (VRT)**: Engineering prescription-driven rate controllers with pulse-width modulation (PWM) nozzles for site-specific fertilizer and herbicide application.
   * **Yield Monitoring & Mapping**: Operating and calibrating impact plate mass flow sensors, optical grain volume sensors, and moisture compensation sensors on combine harvesters.
   * **Agricultural Field Robotics**: Designing path planning and control systems for autonomous tractors and robotic weeders (Lecture 13).

2. **BSc in Irrigation and Water Resources Engineering (BSc IWRE — Year 4)**:
   * **Precision Irrigation Management (Lec 12)**: Designing and operating Variable Rate Irrigation (VRI) systems on center pivots, lateral moves, and automated drip networks.
   * **Crop Water Stress Index (CWSI)**: Using thermal infrared sensors and multispectral UAV/satellite imagery (NDWI, NDVI) to identify early moisture stress and schedule deficit irrigation.
   * **Spatial Soil Moisture Mapping**: Utilizing electromagnetic induction (EMI, e.g., EM38) to map apparent soil electrical conductivity ($EC_a$) and delineate soil water-holding capacity management zones.

3. **BSc in Bioprocess and Post-Harvest Engineering (BSc BPHE — Year 4)**:
   * **Quality at Harvest**: Integrating real-time grain moisture and protein spectrometers directly into harvesting and intake conveyors.
   * **Spatial Harvest Traceability**: Linking batch-level post-harvest quality, aflatoxin susceptibility, and drying energy demand back to geospatial harvest origin coordinates.
   * **Yield Map Data Cleaning**: Applying statistical and geostatistical filters to remove spatial outliers and sensor lag from raw agricultural yield telemetry.

## 🗺️ Syllabus & Lecture Breakdown

| Lecture | Topic | Technical & Applied Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lecture 1** | **Introduction to Precision Agriculture** | Spatial & temporal variability; The 4-stage PA cycle (Sensors $\rightarrow$ Maps $\rightarrow$ Decisions $\rightarrow$ Action). |
| **Lecture 2** | **GIS and GPS / GNSS Basics** | Datums, projections (UTM), GNSS constellations (GPS, Galileo), DGPS, RTK correction. |
| **Lecture 3** | **Remote Sensing in Agriculture** | Electromagnetic spectrum, vegetation spectral reflectance curves, NDVI, SAVI, NDRE, NDWI. |
| **Lectures 4 & 5**| **Proximal Soil Sensors** | Apparent soil electrical conductivity ($EC_a$), electromagnetic induction (EMI), gamma radiometry, pH. |
| **Lecture 6** | **Proximal Plant and Crop Sensors** | Active canopy reflectance sensors (GreenSeeker, Crop Circle), chlorophyll fluorometers. |
| **Lecture 7** | **Data Collection and IoT in PA** | Wireless sensor mesh networks, edge nodes, cellular/LoRa telemetry, cloud ingestion. |
| **Lecture 8** | **Control Systems and VRT** | Prescription map execution, closed-loop rate controllers, PWM solenoid nozzles, injection pumps. |
| **Lecture 9** | **Yield Monitoring and Mapping** | Combine harvester impact plates, radiometric flow sensors, moisture compensation, yield map filtering. |
| **Lecture 10**| **Data Management & Spatial Analysis** | Geostatistics, semivariograms, Ordinary Kriging, IDW interpolation, management zone delineation. |
| **Lecture 11**| **Decision Support Systems & AI in PA** | Crop growth simulation models (DSSAT), rule-based expert systems, predictive machine learning. |
| **Lecture 12**| **Precision Irrigation & Water Management** | Variable Rate Irrigation (VRI), CWSI thermal index, zone-specific irrigation scheduling. |
| **Lecture 13**| **Agricultural Robotics and Automation** | Autonomous field navigation, obstacle avoidance, robotic selective weeders, robotic harvesters. |
| **Lecture 14**| **Economic and Environmental Impacts** | Cost-benefit analysis, chemical reduction, runoff mitigation, carbon footprint accounting. |
| **Lecture 15**| **Future Trends and Frontier Challenges** | Edge AI, autonomous swarm robotics, smallholder mechanization models, data governance. |
